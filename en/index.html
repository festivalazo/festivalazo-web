2026 · 9th edition · El Origen

Four days to go back to the origin: to the body, to play and to the circle. Tents, woods, campfire and acrobatics classes for every level, an hour away from the city.

The experience

Body, fire & circle

01

Classes

Up to five parallel classes per slot: troupe, icarians, whips, hand to hand, flow, acroyoga, handstands and more. From beginner to advanced.

02

Fire & circle

Fire opening, campfire, ritual with dance and singing bowls, variety show, sound journey and full-moon jam.

03

Wellbeing

Sunrise yoga and meditation, Thai massage, body percussion and a spotting & care class.

04

Community

Home-cooked food made with love, group photo, a souvenir and a farewell jam.
